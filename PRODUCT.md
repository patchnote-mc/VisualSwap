# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Minecraft players who investigate, practice, or visualize attribute-swap timing. They use Visual Swap during active play and configure it between sessions to make timing feedback match their preferred items and practice setup.

## Product Purpose

Visual Swap provides precise, client-only visual feedback for swap-hit timing without changing gameplay. Success means players can immediately distinguish a possible swap, a landed swap, and a consecutive chain, then configure those signals without ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

Precise and accurate. The interface should feel native to Minecraft, technically trustworthy, and focused on useful feedback.

## Anti-references

Avoid interfaces that look detached from vanilla Minecraft, decorative effects that obscure timing, unfamiliar replacements for standard controls, and feedback that depends on colour alone.

## Design Principles

- Preserve vanilla Minecraft interaction and visual conventions.
- Make timing relationships explicit and mechanically accurate.
- Keep configuration changes immediately previewable.
- Use consistent terminology between configuration and in-game feedback.
- Preserve focus by keeping completed feedback visible until a genuinely new action supersedes it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation and focus behavior provided by vanilla widgets. Pair colour with shape, labels, or position whenever it communicates state; maintain readable contrast; keep motion functional and restrained; and avoid requiring rapid visual tracking outside the configured timing feedback itself.
