Gotchas summary:
  - (Supplier,NativeImage) ctor uploads in the ctor; no manual upload() needed unless pixels change later.
  - Construct/register on render thread (ctor touches RenderSystem.getDevice()).
  - register() closes any previous texture under the same id — re-registering on screen re-open is safe;
    never reuse the old DynamicTexture/NativeImage after that.
  - DynamicTexture owns its NativeImage; closing the texture (or TextureManager.release) frees the image.
  - Sampler is NEAREST + repeat (DynamicTexture.createTexture :50) — no linear filtering on scaled draws.
