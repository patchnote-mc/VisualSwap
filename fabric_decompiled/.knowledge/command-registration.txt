  CommandRegistrationCallback.EVENT.register((dispatcher, buildContext, selection) -> { ... })
  Callback sig: register(CommandDispatcher<CommandSourceStack> dispatcher,
                         CommandBuildContext buildContext,

Register from the "main" ModInitializer.onInitialize() (runs both SP integrated + dedicated).
